# Rubin research coverage matrix

All rows are **planned research questions**, not statements about the product. No implementation details are assumed from the project title.

| Planned page | Questions to resolve | General prerequisite | Evidence gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| rubin_overview | What exact products/variants and release states are documented? | 00, 32 | Dated official product identity and status |
| logic_manufacturing | What logic technology, organization or foundry details are actually disclosed? | 07, 17, 19 | Explicit disclosure; process capability alone is insufficient |
| memory | What interface generation, capacity, bandwidth and stack organization are documented? | 23, 24 | Variant-specific specification with units/conditions; vendor assignments separate |
| advanced_packaging | What package architecture and named implementation are public? | 25 | Product-linked evidence; avoid deducing a subvariant from a brand family |
| substrate | Which construction or routing facts are available? | 26 | Explicit implementation evidence; otherwise unknown |
| power | Which power numbers apply to chip, module, server or rack? | 28 | Measurement boundary and operating condition |
| cooling | What cooling arrangement is disclosed at each boundary? | 27 | Official system documentation with configuration |
| system_architecture | How are GPUs, CPUs, links, modules and racks organized? | 29–32 | Dated block diagrams and exact product scope |
| manufacturing_supply_chain | Which vendor relationships are expressly documented? | 33–35 | Named evidence; ecosystem presence is not a design win |
| manufacturing_bottlenecks | What constraints are documented versus inferred? | 21, 25, 36 | Capacity/time/yield definitions and dated evidence |
| known_vs_unknown | Which claims remain unresolvable or contradictory? | All above | Claim register with alternative explanations and resolution evidence |

Raw feedstock and individual fab recipes may be undisclosed even where broad logic or packaging details are public. Connect general chapters to this boundary instead of implying traceability to a particular mine or reactor.
