# Glossary schema

Each entry uses a stable explicit anchor, term/acronym, expansion, definition, intuitive meaning, purpose/why it matters, canonical explanation link, related terms and source IDs when making a technical claim. One definition per concept; disambiguate overloaded terms such as yield, node, substrate and package.

Initial required term queue: ALD, BEOL, CD, CDU, CMP, CVD, DRC, DRAM, DUV, EUV, FEOL, FinFET, GAA, HBM, KGD, LVS, MOL, MOSFET, OPC, PCB, PDK, PECVD, RDL, RTL, SPC and TSV. Add symbols and terms during article drafting rather than publishing uncited definitions now.

Acronyms are expanded at first use in each standalone article even when they also have glossary entries. Glossary entries link back to full explanations; full articles link to relevant entries.
