# Maintenance and audit protocol

This is a workflow, not a configured scheduled automation. No external messages or recurring jobs are enabled by Phase 0.

## Review triggers

| Content | Proposed review cadence / trigger |
|---|---|
| Fundamental mechanisms | Annual check or whenever a substantive correction is reported |
| Standards and tool/process capabilities | New cited edition, major product change or six-month review |
| Company roles, capacity and financials | New relevant filing/disclosure; at least quarterly while actively used |
| Rubin specifications and availability | New official documentation, revision or announcement; monthly while the case study is active |
| Links and graph structure | Every content change and repository audit |

Cadences are editorial defaults, not promises that automatic research will occur. Never mark a claim current merely because a scheduled date passed.

## Update procedure

Find affected source/claim IDs; read the new evidence; compare exact variants, dates and conditions; update claim and article wording; retain unresolved conflicts; refresh only actual review/access dates; run structural checks; record a small corrective commit.

## Phase 12 audit record

Report broken links; unsupported/missing citations; inconsistent terminology and missing glossary entries; duplicate explanations; orphaned articles; uncited/unlicensed graphics; unit/model mistakes; contradictions; stale product claims; unsupported company roles; economics mixed with engineering; and overconfident Rubin statements. For each finding record severity, path/claim ID, evidence, proposed fix, owner and status.

A passed structural script is only one part of the audit. Review a claim's source passage, each diagram's rights, and the reader's complete physical/economic path. Publish the scope and limitations of the audit rather than reporting generic perfection.
