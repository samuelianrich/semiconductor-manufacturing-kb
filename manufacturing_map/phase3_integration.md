# Phase 3 integration-map guide

The [device/MOL view](device_integration.md) shows planar, FinFET and nanosheet integration as alternatives. Each consumes a qualified starting wafer and produces an abstract compatible device-terminal state. Their common output expresses an interface, not identical physical structures or three simultaneous inputs. Exact upstream wafer options and device recipes are outside this aggregate teaching view.

The [BEOL view](beol_integration.md) follows a representative copper trench/via module through cavity formation, barrier/liner/seed, fill and CMP. The final operation explicitly aggregates additional level repetitions and terminal finishing. It ends at a fabricated wafer with test pending. It does not manufacture a known-good die merely by changing a label.

Repeated levels should become distinct operation and wafer-state instances when later work supplies their actual integration requirements. No self-loop or artificial cycle is needed to describe a finite manufactured stack. The original [Phase 2 oxide route](oxide_pattern_transfer.md) remains a separate teaching route.

`FAM-0200` groups integration functions. Equipment edges name selected process functions, not exclusive tool lists or suppliers. Material/geometry and reliability concepts link into the [entity register](process_nodes.md#fam-0200); method-specific inspection and electrical qualification must be added before assigning guaranteed detection or acceptance.

The original aggregate logic path includes test and die preparation and remains planned. Phase 3's researched fabrication subroutes do not silently promote that larger scope or the memory/package/system paths. All newly reviewed assertions have source locators in [evidence links](data/evidence_links.csv); route composition is author synthesis, not a production flow disclosed by one source.
